---
title: "Impersonation"
description: "Superuser-minted, non-refreshable tokens for another record."
sidebar:
  order: 7
---

`POST .../impersonate/{id}` (superuser-only) mints a session token for
another record. It's deliberately non-refreshable — a one-shot loan the
impersonated record can't extend into a standing session on its own.
