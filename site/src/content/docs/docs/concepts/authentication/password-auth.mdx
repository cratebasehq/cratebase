---
title: "Password auth"
description: "Registration, auth-with-password, and auth rate limiting."
sidebar:
  order: 2
---

Registration is `POST .../records` on an auth collection with
`password`/`passwordConfirm`. Signing in is
`POST .../auth-with-password` with an `identity` (not necessarily an
email — whatever `authOptions.identityField` names) and `password`.
`AUTH_RATE_LIMIT_ENABLED` (on by default) rate-limits
`auth-with-password` and the `request-*`/`confirm-*` email/OTP flows per
client IP; `auth-refresh` is deliberately excluded from that limit.
