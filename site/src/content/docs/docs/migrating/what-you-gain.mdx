title: "What you gain"
description: "The honest list: Postgres, speed, AI endpoints \u2014 and what you don't get yet."
---

Migrating to Cratebase gets you a first-class, zero-code-change Postgres
option, the [benchmark numbers](/docs/benchmarks/) on the categories that
were measured, the [AI endpoints](/docs/ai/overview/) (vector search,
LLM chat gateway, MCP), [schema-as-code](/docs/extending/schema-as-code/),
[teams](/docs/concepts/authentication/teams/), an
[audit log](/docs/deploy/logs/), and [Prometheus metrics](/docs/deploy/metrics/).

Stated just as plainly, what PocketBase still has that Cratebase doesn't:
a larger ecosystem and years in production. This page won't pretend
otherwise — see [Compatibility](/docs/migrating/compatibility/) for
what's actually been verified before deciding.
